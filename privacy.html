Privacy notice

Last updated 19 November 2026

Who we are

Seravyx is a student project by group SE16 at the Informatics Institute of Technology, Sri Lanka, in partnership with the University of Westminster. You can contact us at hello@seravyx.com.

What we collect

When you join the pilot waitlist we collect your name, your email address or mobile number, and whether you are a patient, a caregiver or a clinician. We do not collect any health information through this website.

Why we collect it

Only to contact you about the Seravyx pilot and to understand who is interested in it. We will not sell your details or use them for advertising.

Legal basis

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form. We handle your details in line with Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022.

How long we keep it

Until the pilot has started or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first, and no longer than 12 months.

Who can see it

Only the five members of the project team. Sign-ups are stored with the form provider the team uses to receive them.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by emailing hello@seravyx.com. You can also withdraw your consent at any time.

Medical disclaimer

Seravyx is a prototype and is not a medical device. It does not give medical advice. In an emergency, call 1990.
